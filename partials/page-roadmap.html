Roadmap

Five phases. Each one compounds the last.

Nothing ships before the layer it depends on. The order is the plan.

01
Live now
Memory

Permanent, provable recall is the first thing an agent needs and the first thing Lumera shipped. It is live now on SuperNodes, and it is what every later phase is written against. Builders get a memory layer today; the network gets its first real workload.

02
Next
Identity

Next, memory attaches to an identity the agent owns rather than an account it rents. That identity carries the record of what the agent has done, so reputation travels with it between frameworks and operators.

03
Then
Verifiable execution

Then the work itself becomes checkable. A job leaves a receipt describing what ran and what it cost, and a second node can re-run the step to confirm the answer. Enterprises get an audit trail; honest operators get a way to prove they are honest.

04
Then
Composition

As the network matures, several agents can be pointed at one job, each step provable on its own. That is when multi-model workflows stop being a prototype and start being something you can put in front of a customer.

05
Then
Autonomous agents

Finally the delegated agent: its own identity, its own budget, its own receipt trail, and a reputation it earned rather than claimed. You give it a goal and a spending limit, and you can check every decision it made afterwards.

Where this ends up

The delegated agent.

An agent with an identity of its own, a budget you set, a receipt for every step it took, and a reputation it has to earn. You hand it a goal, not a script.
